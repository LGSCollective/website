Leadership

The organization's leadership team is responsible for guiding the mission, supporting members, and overseeing strategic initiatives. LGS Collective is led by a team of passionate volunteers who believe in giving back to the community and creating opportunities to support kids and families. Our leadership team brings together different backgrounds, skills, and experiences, but we all share the same goal: to build a strong organization, support our volunteers, and make a positive impact in the communities we serve. We work together, keep things moving, and are always looking for new ways to grow LGS Collective and make a difference.
