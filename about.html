About LGS Collective

LGS Collective was formed in 2026 with a simple goal: to provide meaningful volunteer opportunities that further the mission and support community growth.  We’re a group of volunteers who believe in giving back, having fun, and making a difference —one event at a time. 

Through collaboration and fellowship, our organization seeks to create meaningful opportunities that support growth and improve the communities we serve.
